Community Service Project (CSP)

About AgriSetu

Connecting Farmers. Empowering Labourers.

1. Project Background & Social Problem

Agriculture is the lifeblood of rural India, yet smallholder farmers frequently suffer critical crop losses during harvest and sowing seasons due to acute labour shortages. Simultaneously, agricultural workers and daily-wage labourers face severe underemployment and loss of income because they lack direct visibility into which nearby farms require assistance.

Traditionally, this information gap has been filled by unregulated middlemen and labour contractors who extract 15% to 30% of the worker's hard-earned daily wages while providing zero transparency or safety guarantees.

AgriSetu (The Agricultural Bridge) was built as a Community Service Project (CSP) to dismantle these barriers by providing a direct, transparent, and mobile-accessible digital link between farmers and workers.

2. Core Engineering Principles

  • Concurrency-Safe Worker Allocation: Mathematical guarantee (Remaining Vacancies = Required Workers - Confirmed Workers) enforced with database row-level locking to eliminate overbooking and ghost assignments.
  • Contact Privacy by Default: Labourers' and farmers' mobile numbers are protected against spam and unsolicited scraping; mutual contact details are only revealed when a formal work assignment is confirmed.
  • Inclusive Multilingual Access: Complete support for Telugu, Hindi, and English so that local rural workers can navigate the platform in their native language.
  • Zero Exploitation: Transparent daily wages, meal arrangements, transport options, and work hours disclosed upfront before anyone applies.

3. Safety and Community Standards

Farmers posting on AgriSetu must provide clean drinking water, emergency first aid kits, and accurate working hour expectations. Mutual post-work reviews establish trust, while administrative dispute moderation ensures immediate resolution of any grievances.
