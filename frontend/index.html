How AgriSetu Works

A transparent, structured 4-step workflow connecting rural supply and demand.

🚜 For Farmers

  1. Post job details, required number of workers, and transparent daily wages.
  2. Review verified local labourer applications with skills & experience.
  3. Accept workers with real-time vacancy tracking and zero overbooking.
  4. Confirm completed work, reveal contact numbers, and share mutual ratings.

🌾 For Agricultural Labourers

  1. Search verified local jobs by village, crop category, and wage.
  2. Apply with 1-click without revealing your phone number publicly.
  3. Receive instant notifications when your application is accepted.
  4. Access direct farm contact, complete work, and build your digital reputation.

Our Agricultural Employment Mission

AgriSetu connects farmers facing seasonal labour shortages directly with skilled agricultural workers looking for reliable local employment. By eliminating exploitative intermediaries, offering Telugu, Hindi, and English access, and enforcing strict contact privacy, AgriSetu empowers rural livelihoods through transparent technology.
